- Live address: https://skylah-disqus-clarity-nu.vercel.app/
- Who it is for, and the one job it does for them: For people in Singapore about to commute or head outdoors, it shows the official NEA 2-hour forecast for their own neighbourhood and a plain tip on what to do.
- Health check from Step 1, on Friday 25 September 2026, about 6:13 pm SGT: `/api/health` returned `200 {"keyConfigured":true,"upstreamAnswered":true,"upstreamStatus":200}` in about 640 ms. `/api/weather` returned `200` with 47 areas (all "Cloudy"), valid 6.00 pm to 8.00 pm, `updateTimestamp` 18:06:31+08:00, served from the Vercel cache (`x-vercel-cache: STALE`, age 28 s, about 20 ms).
- Devices and browsers I used for this evaluation: Chromium (Claude desktop in-app browser) at 375×812 with mobile emulation, at about 800 px wide, and at 1366×800 laptop width. ## 2. My findings

### Finding 1
- Where: https://skylah-disqus-clarity-nu.vercel.app/, Forecast screen, forecast card and "A little tip" card, once the 2-hour window has passed
- What I did, what I saw: I loaded the page at 6:13 pm (valid 6–8 pm) and moved the page clock forward 3 hours. Nothing changed until I tapped something. Then an amber note appeared: "The latest forecast timestamp from data.gov.sg has expired". Big "Cloudy" was still shown as the current forecast. The tip changed to "Suggestions appear automatically once a current forecast is confirmed." Nothing appeared automatically. The page never fetches again (`/api/weather` is called once, on load), and the note has no Refresh button. The only way out is a browser reload, which also resets my area to City (Finding 2).
- Which heuristic: 1, Visibility of system status (it also touches 9: the note does not say how to recover)
- Screen or system: Both. The screen could fix part of this with what it already has: the component already receives `onRetry`, so the expired note could show a "Get latest forecast" button, and the misleading "automatically" line could be removed. Refetching on a timer, or when the tab becomes visible again, is system work.
- Severity, and why: 3. Frequency drove it. The app's own tip says "check again before heading out", so going back to a tab that is already open is the normal use. Each time, the person gets an old forecast shown as current, or a dead end.
- The repair: A forecast past its valid time is never shown as current. The page gets fresh data when it comes back into view, the expired state has one clear "Get latest forecast" button, and the page promises only what it actually does.

### Finding 2
- Where: https://skylah-disqus-clarity-nu.vercel.app/, Forecast screen, area selector
- What I did, what I saw: I picked Woodlands, then reloaded. The page came back on City. local Storage is empty, so no choice is saved. In the code, every successful load (including Retry after an error) sets the area back to "City" whenever City is in the data, so a Retry also loses my area.
- Which heuristic: 7, Flexibility and efficiency of use
- Screen or system: System. Remembering the choice (localStorage or the URL) and stopping the fetch handler from overwriting it are state logic. The screen alone cannot fix it.
- Severity, and why: 3. Frequency and persistence drove it. A regular checks the same area several times a day and pays this cost every visit. For the 41 areas not in the quick-switch row, that means scrolling a 47-item dropdown each time.
- The repair: A returning visitor lands straight on their last area, and Retry never changes the area they chose.

### Finding 3
- Where: https://skylah-disqus-clarity-nu.vercel.app/, bottom navigation (Forecast / All Areas / Feedback) and the All Areas search box
- What I did, what I saw: I opened All Areas from Forecast and pressed Back. There was no in-app history (`history.length` stayed at 1, and the URL stays `/` on every tab), so Back leaves SkyLah. I typed "Orchard" into the All Areas search, switched to Forecast and back, and the search box was empty again. No area or tab can be bookmarked or shared as a link.
- Which heuristic: 3, User control and freedom
- Screen or system: System. It needs routing (a URL per tab and area, such as `/?area=Woodlands#areas`). The screen cannot fix it alone.
- Severity, and why: 3. Impact drove it. On a phone, Back or the swipe-back gesture is the reflex for "go back to my forecast", and it throws the person out of the app entirely.
- The repair: Back from All Areas or Feedback returns to the Forecast screen with the same area. Each view has its own address, so it can be reloaded, bookmarked or shared.

### Finding 4
- Where: https://skylah-disqus-clarity-nu.vercel.app/, All Areas screen, "Search Singapore area" box
- What I did, what I saw: I searched "Orchard", a place everyone in Singapore knows. The result was "No Singapore forecast areas matching 'Orchard'." There was no suggestion and no hint that forecasts only exist for 47 NEA areas, so the visitor has to already know which NEA area covers where they are going.
- Which heuristic: 2, Match between system and the real world
- Screen or system: Mostly the screen. The empty state could explain "Forecasts are by NEA area. Try the nearest town, e.g. Tanglin or City." Matching landmarks and MRT stations to areas properly needs a lookup table (system).
- Severity, and why: 2. Impact drove it: it is a dead end, but the person can recover by guessing another name. Frequency is moderate, because many trips are named by landmark or mall, not by NEA town.
- The repair: Searching a common place name either leads to the right forecast area or says plainly how areas are named and suggests the nearest one.

### Finding 5
- Where: https://skylah-disqus-clarity-nu.vercel.app/ on a phone (375×812), Forecast screen, first view
- What I did, what I saw: I opened the app on a phone-sized screen. Most of the first screen is the area picker: a dropdown plus six quick-switch buttons. "Cloudy" sits at the bottom edge, and the tip that answers "what should I do" is entirely below the fold. There are four separate ways to choose an area (dropdown, quick switch, header pill, All Areas list).
- Which heuristic: 8, Aesthetic and minimalist design
- Screen or system: Screen. It can reorder and collapse with what it already has.
- Severity, and why: 2. Frequency drove it: every phone visit starts with a scroll to reach the answer. The impact per visit is small.
- The repair: On a phone, the first screen shows the area, the forecast and its valid time, and the tip. Changing the area is one compact control.

### Finding 6
- Where: https://skylah-disqus-clarity-nu.vercel.app/, All Areas screen, then after tapping an area
- What I did, what I saw: I scrolled to Woodlands in All Areas and tapped it. The app switched to Forecast but kept the old scroll position (scrollY about 690), so the forecast card and the "Woodlands" heading were off-screen. I saw only the tip card and the small header pill. It was not obvious that the change happened.
- Which heuristic: 1, Visibility of system status
- Screen or system: Screen. Scrolling to the top (or to the forecast card) when switching tabs is a front-end fix.
- Severity, and why: 2. Frequency drove it: this happens every time someone picks an area from the list, which is the main way to reach the 41 areas not in the quick-switch row. It is recoverable by scrolling up.
- The repair: After choosing an area, the person immediately sees that area's name and forecast at the top of the screen.

### Finding 7
- Where: https://skylah-disqus-clarity-nu.vercel.app/, Forecast screen, "A little tip for your day" when the forecast is Cloudy
- What I did, what I saw: The app calls itself a "Commuter & outdoor decision helper". For Cloudy, the "Just me" tip is "Cloudy skies ahead ☁️ Check again before heading out." It does not say whether to bring an umbrella. It hands the decision back and asks the person to check again, which runs into Finding 1.
- Which heuristic: 2, Match between system and the real world (the words do not answer the question the visitor came with)
- Screen or system: Screen, as copy and tip rules. The forecast text it needs is already on the page.
- Severity, and why: 2. Impact drove it: the one job ("should I bring an umbrella or go out") is only half done in a very common weather state.
- The repair: Every tip gives a concrete decision ("No umbrella needed until 8 pm", "Carry one, showers likely") and the time it holds until.

### Finding 8
- Where: https://skylah-disqus-clarity-nu.vercel.app/, Forecast screen, tip card with "For my child" / "For my beloved ones" selected, on a phone
- What I did, what I saw: The label on the tip reads "for for my child" and "for for my beloved ones" (the word is doubled). On a 375 px screen the third option is cut off as "For my belo…".
- Which heuristic: 4, Consistency and standards (the copy does not match the control it echoes)
- Screen or system: Screen. It is string and layout only.
- Severity, and why: 1. Low impact: purely cosmetic, although it is seen on every use of those options and makes the app look unfinished.
- The repair: The label reads "for my child" once, and all three options fit on a phone without truncating.

### Finding 9
- Where: https://skylah-disqus-clarity-nu.vercel.app/, header "📍 City" pill
- What I did, what I saw: The pill in the top right looks like a status badge showing my area. Tapping it jumps to All Areas. Nothing in its look says it is a button or where it goes. Elsewhere, choosing an area is done with a dropdown or buttons that stay on the Forecast screen.
- Which heuristic: 4, Consistency and standards
- Screen or system: Screen.
- Severity, and why: 1. Low impact: nothing is lost, and people who never tap it are unaffected.
- The repair: Either it clearly reads as a control ("Change area ›") or it is a plain label.

### Finding 10
- Where: https://skylah-disqus-clarity-nu.vercel.app/, first load (Network panel) and footer notice
- What I did, what I saw: On first load, before I did anything, the page requested `clarity.ms/tag/…` and `clarity.js` and sent several `k.clarity.ms/collect` beacons. The only notice is small footer text: "By using this page you agree…". There is no choice to decline.
- Which heuristic: 3, User control and freedom
- Screen or system: System. Script loading has to wait for a consent state. The screen alone cannot stop requests that were already sent.
- Severity, and why: 2. Persistence drove it: it affects every visitor on every visit, and they cannot opt out. It does not block the forecast task.
- The repair: Session recording starts only after the visitor has said yes, and saying no costs them nothing.

### Finding 11
- Where: https://skylah-disqus-clarity-nu.vercel.app/ error states (read from the shipped JS; I could not force the upstream to fail) and unknown addresses
- What I did, what I saw: The 404 state reads "The weather service endpoint was not found (404)." That is a status code and a developer word ("endpoint"), with a Retry button that will fail again. Separately, a made-up address (`/nonexistent-page`) returns 200 and shows the normal forecast with no sign that the link was wrong. (The timeout and unreachable messages are fine: they say what happened and give a Retry.)
- Which heuristic: 9, Help users recognize, diagnose, and recover from errors
- Screen or system: Screen for the wording. System for the fallback route.
- Severity, and why: 1. Low frequency: the 404 state should almost never happen in production.
- The repair: Every error says in plain words what happened and what the visitor can do next, with no status codes.

## 3. My predictions
1. The three findings I expect my groupmates to raise, and the severity I expect them to give each:
   - Finding 3 (Back leaves the app): they will give it **3**. The assignment tells everyone to press Back mid-task, and on SkyLah it throws you out at once.
   - Finding 5 (answer below the fold on phone): they will give it **2**. It is the first thing anyone sees on their phone, before touching anything.
   - Finding 8 ("for for my child"): they will give it **1**. A doubled word is easy to spot and quick to write up, so most people will log it even though it barely matters.
2. The heuristic I think my product breaks worst: **1, Visibility of system status.** SkyLah's whole value is "is this forecast current for my area right now". It shows old forecasts as current when a tab is left open (Finding 1) and hides the result of choosing an area (Finding 6).
3. The finding that would show my own evaluation was wrong: every one of the 47 areas was "Cloudy" throughout my evaluation, so I never saw a rain, showers or thundery-showers state. If a groupmate tests on Monday while any area shows showers and reports that the icon, the tip or the "for my child" advice is wrong, missing or unsafe for rain, and rates it 3 or 4, then my evaluation missed the states that matter most for a weather decision tool. A second sign: someone on a real phone on 3G, or after a cold start, reports a blank or frozen first screen. I only tested emulated widths on a fast connection.
